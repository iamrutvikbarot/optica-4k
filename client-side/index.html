Architecture & Mathematical Foundations

Rather than relying on closed-source cloud models with recurring subscriptions, Optica compiles classical digital signal processing algorithms directly into the browser via WebAssembly:

01
Zero Server Compute

All frame interpolation is calculated by your client machine's processor. No cloud servers are loaded.

02
Lanczos-3 Sinc Filter

Calculates high-order sinc interpolation curves to reconstruct sharp diagonal lines and high-frequency details.

03
Absolute Confidentiality

Your video stream never transmits across any network. 100% processed and stored in client-side memory.

04
YouTube VP9 Codec

Upscaling to 1440p forces YouTube to assign its premium VP9/AV01 encoder instead of blurry 1080p AVC1.
