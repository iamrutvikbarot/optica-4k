How Non-AI Video Enhancement Works

Unlike expensive AI models that cost \$10–\$50/month and hallucinate artificial textures or face distortions, pure mathematical signal processing provides artifact-free, 100% stable enhancement with zero cost:

01

3D Spatial-Temporal Denoise

Cleans digital sensor noise, ISO grain, and compression macroblocks across preceding and succeeding frames before upscaling.

02

Lanczos-3 Sinc Resampling

Interpolates new pixels using a windowed sinc mathematical function, preserving high-frequency edges far better than standard bicubic.

03

AMD Contrast Adaptive Sharpening

Industry-standard CAS dynamically sharpens soft texture areas while preserving flat gradients and preventing halo rings around high-contrast edges.

04
